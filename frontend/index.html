How this tracker works

  • Enter cumulative readings.Use the total shown on the water meter, not an estimate of daily usage.
  • Usage is calculated.Current meter value minus the previous dated value gives interval usage.
  • Keep dates consistent.Record at a similar time where possible. A multi-day interval is not the same as one day.
  • Your data is in Supabase.Entries are saved in the configured PostgreSQL database.
